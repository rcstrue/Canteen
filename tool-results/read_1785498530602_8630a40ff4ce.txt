    32→### Business Logic Implemented
    33→
    34→**Stock Movement (POST):**
    35→- PURCHASE: adds quantity to currentStock, updates lastPurchasePrice and avgCost (weighted average)
    36→- CONSUMPTION: subtracts quantity from currentStock (min 0)
    37→- WASTAGE: subtracts quantity from currentStock (min 0)
    38→- ADJUSTMENT: sets currentStock to the quantity value
    39→
    40→**Daily Meals (POST):**
    41→- Looks up recipe and its ingredients
    42→- Calculates consumption = (mealsServed / baseServings) × ingredient.quantity
    43→- Creates CONSUMPTION stock movements for each ingredient
    44→- Updates ingredient currentStock
    45→
    46→**Purchases (POST):**
    47→- Creates purchase record with items
    48→- Creates PURCHASE stock movements for each item
    49→- Updates ingredient currentStock, lastPurchasePrice, avgCost (weighted average)
    50→- Calculates total amount from items
    51→
    52→**Dashboard (GET):**
    53→- Total food cost today/week/month (from PURCHASE stock movements)
    54→- Cost per meal = total food cost / total meals served
    55→- Low stock alerts (ingredients where currentStock < minStock)
    56→- Top consuming ingredients (from CONSUMPTION movements)
    57→- Today's meals served breakdown
    58→- Monthly expense summary and breakdown
    59→
    60→**Reports (GET):**
    61→- Support period filter: today/week/month/custom (startDate + endDate)
    62→- Cost report: total cost, cost per meal, category breakdown, daily trend, operating expenses
    63→- Consumption report: ingredient usage, wastage analysis, category breakdown
    64→- Variance report: theoretical vs actual consumption with status classification
